---

## 📜 Program Overview  

The **Digital Egypt Builders Initiative (DEBI)** is a groundbreaking initiative launched by the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) in September 2020. It is part of the "Building the Egyptian Person" pillar within MCIT's strategy for constructing a digital Egypt. DEBI aims to empower Egypt's next generation to become global leaders in the digital world, capable of driving Egypt's digital transformation.

---
The initiative offers a free scholarship to exceptional Egyptian graduates, equipping them with the skills needed for digital transformation. 
In addition to technical expertise, participants undergo:

- Leadership and administrative skills training.
- Hybrid education combining in-person sessions and e-learning.
- Workshops and training by local and international companies.
- Certification upon completing technical and leadership training.

## Program Structure

 **Digital Egypt Builders Initiative - Professional Program (DEBI Professional)**
   - Duration: 8 months
   - Focus: Professional and technical skill development

#### Program Benefits
- Participants gain expertise in AI, data science, and other cutting-edge fields.
- Collaboration with leading international universities and tech companies.
- Exposure to real-world challenges through applied workshops and national projects.
- Development of leadership, language, and administrative skills.

---

## Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Track
This track focuses on applying data science and machine learning techniques to solve real-world problems across industries such as healthcare, agriculture, communications, and education.

### Educational Objectives

- Acquire practical experience in solving problems using AI.
- Master topics like machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing.
- Develop skills for collecting, analyzing, and processing data.
- Implement and manage large-scale AI projects.
- Design reliable intelligent systems and competitive AI solutions.
- Learn to define infrastructure for big data storage and processing.
